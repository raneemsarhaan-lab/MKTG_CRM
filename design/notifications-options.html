Where should notifications live?

Today the Board header has one Inbox button (spec 006). It replaced the old bell and holds what needs you (reviews and your tasks), mentions, team activity and what you published. Spec 012 adds a record of every event: comments, replies, moves into your stage, attachments, assignments, mentions and due dates, with read state and grouping. Pick an option below, then click the header buttons in the mock. The task names and people are examples.

A toast appears in the corner for 5 seconds, and the count goes up. Toasts are off while Do not disturb is on, and for the task you have open.
