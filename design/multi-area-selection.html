How to select

  • Ctrl + click a cell: adds it. It becomes the active cell.
  • Ctrl + drag: adds a block.
  • Ctrl + click a column letter or row number: adds the whole column or row.
  • Ctrl + click a selected cell: removes just that cell. Ctrl + drag over selected cells removes the block.
  • Shift + click or Shift + arrows: resizes the newest area only.
  • Click without Ctrl, or an arrow key: back to one cell.
  • Keyboard only: Shift+F8 turns on Add to selection. Arrows move, Shift+arrows add a new area. Esc ends it.
  • Type, then Ctrl+Enter: the value goes into every selected cell.

Phones and tablets keep today's touch selection.

What tools do with several areas

Every area, one undo

All cell formats, cell styles, borders (each area outlined on its own), Format Painter, Delete, Clear contents / formats / all / comments, Clear rules in selection, Ctrl+Enter, and pasting one copied cell.

When the areas line up

Copy works when every area covers the same rows (placed side by side) or the same columns (stacked). Hide, Resize and Delete rows or columns work when every area is whole rows, or every area is whole columns.

One area only

Cut, pasting a block, Merge, Sort, Filter, Format as Table, Fill Down / Right, Paste Special, New rule, Insert rows / columns, and Board ▾ Create tasks for rows. They are disabled and say why: “Works on one area. Click a cell without Ctrl to start again.”
