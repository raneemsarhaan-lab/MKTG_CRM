  • Selecting: the bar shows when two or more clip rows are selected: click a row number, Shift-click for a run, Ctrl-click to add one, or select cells that cross several rows. Video bands and "+ Clip" rows aren't counted. Esc or ✕ clears.
  • The choices: Stage, Owner (the clip's editor), Reviewer (its task owner), Type, Priority and Due date, with the board's own lists and its date picker. Brand is left out: clips take their video's brand.
  • The board's rules: the same server action as the board's bar. A clip someone can't edit, or a stage move the board doesn't allow, is left as it was and named in the message.
  • Undo: one Undo in the message puts every changed clip back.
  • Drop: asks first ("Drop 3 clips?"), as Drop does for one clip; Undo restores them. Dropped clips stay on the sheet under Show archived.
