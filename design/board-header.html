The Board header, rebuilt in three rows

A working preview of spec 011, drawn from your reference. Everything in the header is clickable: the tabs, search, Inbox, avatar, New task, the brand dropdown, Filters, Board or Sheet, and Display options. Use the bar below to try a phone, Arabic, a member who can't create tasks, and the Inbox in its three states.

Row 1 · Where am I

Pipeline and Content are the page tabs. Search sits in the middle. The Inbox is compact: its summary line ("1 late · 2 reviews waiting") is in the tooltip and read out by screen readers. The badge turns orange when something is late or due today.

Row 2 · This page

"Pipeline" with the purple ticks, and New task on the far end. The greeting stays on My Day. A member who can't create tasks sees no button, and the row keeps its height.

Row 3 · How it's shown

My tasks or Team tasks, one brand dropdown in place of the logo row, and Filters. Board or Sheet sits on the far end, followed by Display options (Show your week, Columns before design). The dark "Board" button that did nothing is gone.

On a phone

Search folds into an icon, and the Inbox becomes an icon with its badge. The toolbar stays one row and scrolls sideways. Every control is at least 44 px tall, and the first column moves up from about 540 px to under 320.
