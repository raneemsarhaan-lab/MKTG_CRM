Where the Sheets live

A new Sheets section in the sidebar. Inside it, folders: Content and Video (the two sheets that are linked to the board) and any folder the team makes. A folder opens like one Google Sheets file, with its sheets as tabs along the bottom. Pick how the folders open; everything is clickable.

View Try: open a folder · click tabs · press + · right-click a tab for Excel’s menu (Insert, Delete, Rename, Move or Copy, Protect, Tab Color, Hide, Unhide, Select All) · drag across cells

How this answers the three open questions

  1. Where the tabs live. Each folder is one workbook with its own tabs. The Content folder's first tab is the Content sheet and the Video folder's is the Video sheet; both are linked to the board (🔗), can't be deleted, and are always first. Next to them, and in any team folder, the team adds blank sheets with +.
  2. Who sees what. Suggested: folders are shared with the whole team, and anyone can add, rename and reorder sheets in them. A folder's menu could also offer Only me for private scratch folders, if you want that.
  3. Who can edit cells on tasks. Suggested: blank sheets are open to everyone. On the linked tabs, the team's own columns are open to everyone, while pipeline fields (title, stage, assignee) keep today's rule (the task's owner, assignee, admins and superusers).
  4. The Board header. Suggested: its Content tab and Sheet view stay, as shortcuts that open the same linked sheets, so nobody's habit breaks.
