One pipeline, media boards, Space stages

Every task runs the same 11 stages. Spaces decide who gets new work and what the stages are called in their own views. The Design and Video boards hold media tasks, linked to a content task or standing alone.

The member holding the role gets new tasks made in the Space. Changing it applies to new tasks only. المسؤول الافتراضي

  • Every task is a pipeline task: 11 stages, or 10 when its brand has C-Check off. The switch applies to new tasks; a task already in C-Check finishes it.
  • A task made in a Space takes that Space's default assignee. The new-task form shows that person, and it can be changed.
  • If several members hold the role, the first in the Space's member list gets it; if none does, the creator does.
  • Simple tasks are gone. Old ones move to To Do, C-In Progress or Published (no celebration, not added to the brand Calendar as new posts).

  • Each Space stage maps to one original stage; every original stage keeps at least one. One Space stage can't cover two.
  • Sketch → Final art changes only this Space: no stage change, no SLA restart, no approval, no move in other Spaces. Anyone in the Space can do it.
