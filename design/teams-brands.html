Spec 021 · Option B, for comparison

Teams × Brands

Brands hold the work, each with the same four Folders: Calendar, Assets, Sheet and Library. Team Spaces hold no tasks of their own. Each one is a lens that pulls its team's work from every brand. A task lives in one brand Folder and shows up in every team Space it belongs to. Click around: the data is sample data.

One task, one home, many team lenses

Open tasks in this sample, by brand and team Space. A task can count in two columns: a LinkedIn post is in Content while it's written and in Design while it's designed.

How this differs from the spec as written

If you choose this option, these are the changes to spec 021.

Spec as written (Option A)

  • Space = a brand
  • Folder = a team inside that brand (Content, Design, Video)
  • A designer working on three brands opens three Design Folders, or builds a view on Everything
  • Status words are set per team Folder, so each brand repeats them
  • Teams can lay out each brand differently

This option (B)

  • Brand = where work lives, always with Calendar, Assets, Sheet and Library. The Calendar is one continuous calendar, not a List per month
  • Team Space = a lens across all brands: no tasks of its own, only a rule for which tasks it shows and who its members are
  • A designer opens one Space and sees every brand's design work
  • Status words are set once per team Space
  • Feature switches stay per brand
  • Hiding work from a team means leaving that team out of the Space, or making a brand Folder private
