Options Excel shows but that need formulas or objects (which these sheets don't have) are shown greyed out, as Excel greys out options that don't apply.

As in Excel

  • The fill handle is the square at the bottom-right of the selection; the pointer turns into a thin black +.
  • While dragging, a grey border shows the target and a pale-yellow tip shows the last value (30).
  • 5, 10 → 15, 20, 25, 30. One cell copies; Ctrl while dragging switches between copying and filling a series.
  • Double-click the handle: fills down as far as the column beside it has data.
  • Drag back inside the selection: the cells turn grey and are cleared on release.
  • Shift+drag: inserts cells instead of overwriting.

Auto Fill Options

  • The icon appears at the bottom-right of the filled range and stays until the next edit.
  • The list depends on what was filled: dates add Fill Days / Weekdays / Months / Years; text and numbers show the first four and Flash Fill.
  • Fill Weekdays skips the workspace weekend (Friday, Saturday by default): 10/12 is followed by 13/12.
  • Choosing another option replaces the fill: still one undo.

Right-drag the fill handle

  • Drag with the right mouse button; on release, Excel's menu appears.
  • Items that don't fit the data are greyed (Days and Weekdays for a month name).

  • Series… opens the Series dialog (next tab).

F5 / Ctrl+G → Special…

  • Go To lists recent references; type B4, A1:D5 or Budget!D20 and press OK.
  • Go To Special has Excel's layout. Notes are the sheet's comments; Constants = filled cells (numbers / text).
  • Formulas, Precedents, Dependents, Current array, Objects, Logicals and Errors are greyed: these sheets have no formulas or objects.
  • The result is a multi-area selection; then type and press Ctrl+Enter to fill them all.

Keys from the same list

  • Shift+Space row · Ctrl+Space column
  • F4 / Ctrl+Y repeat last action
  • Double-click a cell border: jump like Ctrl+arrow
  • Ctrl+; date · Ctrl+Shift+; time
  • Alt+; select visible cells only

Data › Data Validation…

  • Excel's three tabs: Settings, Input Message, Error Alert.
  • Allow: List (Source as text, or a range like =$F$1:$F$3 on the same sheet), Whole number, Decimal, Date, Time, Text length. Custom needs formulas: greyed.
  • Input Message shows a yellow note when a cell is selected.
  • Error Alert: Stop rejects, Warning asks “Continue?”, Information just informs.
  • Team sheets only; Content and Video keep their own pickers.

In the cell

  • As in Excel, the ▼ button shows only beside the selected cell; values look like plain text.
  • Alt+↓ or a click on ▼ opens the list.

  • Pasted and filled values are checked too; the ones refused are counted in a note.

As in Excel

  • Status bar: Average, Count, Sum on the right (right-click it to add Numerical Count, Minimum, Maximum), the view buttons and the zoom slider with − / + and the percentage.
  • Ctrl+wheel zooms 10%–400% in Excel; here 50%–200%. Click the percentage for the Zoom dialog. Kept for you per sheet.
  • Ctrl+F1 collapses the ribbon (our toolbar).
  • Press Alt: KeyTip badges appear under each menu; press the letter. Excel's letters where they match (Insert N, Data A, View W).
