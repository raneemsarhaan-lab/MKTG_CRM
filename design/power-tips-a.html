Drag the handle

  • The small square at the selection's corner. Drag down, up, left or right.
  • A dashed outline shows the target; the tooltip shows the last value.
  • Two values set the step (5, 10 → 15, 20…). One value copies; Ctrl while dragging swaps copy and series.
  • Dates, months (Jan → Feb), days and “Item 3” continue; your team's own lists too.
  • Double-click the handle: fills down to the end of the data beside it.
  • Drag back into the selection: clears those cells. Shift+drag: inserts cells instead of overwriting.
  • One undo step. Content and Video sheets: the same, through each cell's own rules.

Auto Fill Options

  • Stays beside the filled block until the next action.
  • Picking another option redoes the fill that way: still one undo step.
  • Fill weekdays skips the workspace weekend (Friday and Saturday by default), so Thu 10 Dec is followed by Sun 13 Dec.
  • Fill without formatting keeps the target's borders and colours.

F5 → Special

  • F5 or Edit › Go to special…
  • The result is a normal multi-area selection (spec 017): Ctrl+Enter, Clear and formats work on it.
  • Fill blanks from above (tip 68) comes in stage B as a one-click cleanup tool.

Also on the keyboard

  • Shift+Space row · Ctrl+Space column
  • F4 repeats the last action (insert row, a format, a fill)
  • Double-click a cell's edge: jumps like Ctrl+arrow
  • Ctrl+; today · Ctrl+Shift+; now

Ctrl+E Flash Fill

  • Type one or two examples next to the source column, then press Ctrl+E.
  • It fills only when one pattern explains every example: first words, last words, the part after @, initials, joined parts, case changes.
  • Otherwise it fills nothing and says: “Give one more example.”

A list in the cells

  • Each cell shows its value as a chip (in the list's colour) and a ▾.
  • Enter or a click opens the list; typing works as before.
  • Reject: other text is refused with this note. Warn: it's kept, with a note.
  • Pasted, filled and moved values are checked too, and counted in the “not changed” note.
  • Rows inserted inside the range join it.

View

  • Ctrl+wheel or Ctrl+=/− zoom 50–200%; Ctrl+0 resets. Kept for you, per sheet. Also in the status bar.
  • Shift+wheel scrolls sideways.
  • Ctrl+F1 hides or shows the toolbar strip.
  • Press Alt: each menu shows its key letter; Alt+letter opens it.
  • All new keys are added to Help › Keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+/).
