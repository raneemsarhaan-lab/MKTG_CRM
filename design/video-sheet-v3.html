  • Top bar: Sheets / Video · Saved, Board | Sheet, Search, and a lime "+ New clip". The File, Edit, View… menus and the formatting toolbar stay above the sheet as they are today.
  • Stage tabs: All, To do, Editing, In review, Ready to publish, Published, with counts. "Waiting on me": clips you're editing that aren't in review yet, and clips in review that you sign off.
  • Grid: click a Stage, Owner or Reviewer cell and its list opens, as in Excel. "In stage" turns red past the stage's time limit. A video's row folds its clips with ▾.
  • Columns: Clip, Stage, Owner (the clip's editor), Reviewer (the task owner, once the clip is in review), Due, In stage, File. #, Type, Source, File name, In, Out, Length and Published can be switched back on in Columns.
