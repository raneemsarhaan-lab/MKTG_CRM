Content and Video sheets

How the two linked sheets work today: what each row is, what each column holds, who may change what, how Status moves a task, and how they connect to the Design and Video boards. Every row is a real board task, so the sheet and the board are two views of the same work.

1 · What they're for, and where they open

Content sheet

The content plan as a spreadsheet: campaigns (themes), their pillars and their pieces. Used by Content Creators, the Marketing Manager and theme owners.

Video sheet

Every video and its clips, with files, timings and review. Used by editors and the content team.

  • Content Space › Sheet and Video Space › Sheet: a workbook whose first, fixed tab is the linked sheet; blank tabs can sit beside it.
  • Brand › Sheet: All tasks, Content sheet and Video sheet, limited to that brand.
  • Sheets section: the Content and Video folders, with the linked sheet first.
  • From a task panel ("Open sheet") and from a content row's menu ("Open in video sheet").

Linked means an edit in the sheet goes through the board's own save, with the board's rules. A change on the board shows in the sheet straight away.

2 · Rows

Content sheet

  • Theme row: the campaign, with its owner, frequency and audience.
  • Pillar rows under it, each with its pieces; pieces without a pillar follow; then + Piece.
  • A piece is a task in that theme, ordered by publishing date, then due date. A Video piece opens to show its clips.
  • New pieces start at To Do. The theme's owner owns it; whoever added it (if not the owner or an admin) is its assignee.
  • Paste plan: paste rows from the Content Library; a preview sorts them into New, Matched by Task Link, Already in the sheet, and Problems. Pasting the same rows again adds nothing.
  • Archive hides a piece from the board and greys it here; Restore brings it back to its stage.

Video sheet

  • Each video (a Video task) is a band: name, clip count, progress, stage mix and due date.
  • Its clips (subtasks) follow, ordered by clip number (01, 02…), then + Clip.
  • Clip numbers come from the file name (NN_SOURCE_in-out.mp4), the # column or Paste clips.
  • A new clip is a Reel at To Do, owned by whoever adds it. + Video is for admins and superusers.
  • File: upload (up to 200 MB with storage), paste a link, preview, or remove with Undo.
  • Drop works like Archive.

4 · Status and stages

To Do→C-In Progress→C-Review→C-Check→Ready For Design→D-In Progress→D-Check→F-Check→Ready to Publish→Scheduled→Published
  • Change it from the menu (Next, Back to, Skip to) or by typing a stage: the Space's names, the sheet's own words (Writing, Content Review… / Script, Editing, Edit Review…) or the board names.
  • Who may move it: whoever holds the current stage. Review stages (shaded) belong to a role: C-Review and F-Check to the Marketing Manager, C-Check to the Managing Director, D-Check to the Brand Director. Other stages belong to the task's assignee or owner. Admins and superusers can move any task.
  • Brands with C-Check switched off have 10 stages.
  • Required fields: a move that crosses C-Review with Caption or TOV empty is refused: "Caption is required before C-Review". The same rule holds for the menu, typing, bulk moves and the board.
  • Moving a piece to Published asks for its Published link (and LinkedIn link for a Post).

5 · Editing rules

Who edits what

  • Task fields (title, type, assignee, pillar, dates, links, files, clip details, Caption, TOV): the task's owner, its assignee, admins and superusers. Anyone else sees "read only".
  • Custom columns, formatting and comments: any member, on a live row.

How

  • Click and type replaces; Enter, F2 or double-click edits.
  • Paste fills cells and counts what it skipped; a table on a theme row opens Paste plan; clip file names open Paste clips.
  • Fill handle, Ctrl+;, Alt+↓ pick list. Undo and redo follow the same rules.
  • Bulk bar (2+ rows): Content: Stage, Assignee, Type, Pillar, Date, Archive. Video: Stage, Owner, Reviewer, Type, Priority, Due, Drop. One Undo reverses it.

6 · Formatting and comments

  • Fills, fonts, number formats and colour rules are kept per task and column, so they follow the task through sorting, filtering and archiving.
  • Sort and filter work inside each theme or video and never change the board's order.
  • Cell comments are threads with @mentions, resolve and reopen. Paste Special: values, formats, comments, transpose.

7 · Links to the Design and Video boards

  • At Ready For Design a box creates one linked task per board, once: Video and Reel go to the Video board, the rest to Design (set per type). It picks a person for each.
  • Link task cell: type to find an unlinked Design or Video task, or create one. Each link shows the task's name and board, with ⇄ to change and × to unlink. No new links from F-Check on.
  • While linked tasks haven't passed D-Check, the content row's Status follows them (the earliest of their stages). Its menu offers Ready For Design to F-Check, and moving it moves them, with their own permissions. Once every linked task passes D-Check, the row moves to F-Check on its own; after that the linked tasks follow the row.

8 · Shared or private

Shared by everyone

Column layout, custom columns and values, task fields, row heights, the sheet's sort, filter, group and words, formatting, colour rules and comments.

Per person or device

Mine (resets each visit), Show archived / Show dropped (remembered on the device), collapsed themes and videos.

9 · Open points

Where the specs and the code differ, or a decision is still needed:

Clips and required fields. Clips are created as Reels, and Reels require Caption and TOV before C-Review, so every clip needs both. Should clips use their own type (e.g. "Clip") without TOV?
Task field columns in the drawer. Caption, Channels and TOV always sit at the end of the Content and Video sheets and can't be hidden or reordered from Columns yet.
"Waiting for Caption". When a linked row is held at D-Check because a required field is empty, the cards and the sheet show the red "missing" words, but the row doesn't yet say it is waiting.
Linked-row permissions. The Status menu offers stages by the row's own holder, while the server checks the linked tasks' holders, so typing a stage can succeed where the menu offered nothing.
+ Video for users. The button is hidden from regular users, although the server lets them add a video they own.
Older spec text. Spec 008's per-member column sets (Editing, Copy, Full) became one shared set; spec 009 said whoever adds a piece owns it (the theme owner does); spec 010 said built-in columns can't be deleted (they can be hidden everywhere).
