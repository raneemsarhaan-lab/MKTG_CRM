  • Selecting: the bar shows when two or more piece rows are selected. Theme rows, pillar rows, "+ Piece" rows and archived pieces aren't counted. ✕ clears it.
  • The choices: Stage (the Content sheet's names, the board's name beside each), Assignee, Type, Pillar and Date (the due date), with the board's own lists and date picker.
  • Pillar: lists the pillars of the selected pieces' theme, plus "No pillar". When the pieces come from more than one theme, Pillar is greyed out with a tip: "Pick pieces from one theme".
  • The board's rules: the same server rules as the board's bar. A piece someone can't edit, or a stage move the board doesn't allow, is left as it was, and the message says how many weren't changed.
  • Archive: asks first ("Archive 3 pieces?"), as Archive does for one piece; Undo restores them. Archived pieces stay under Show archived.
  • Undo: one Undo in the message puts every changed piece back.
