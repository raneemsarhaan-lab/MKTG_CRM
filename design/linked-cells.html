Linked sheets get the Content sheet's cells

Spec 019 design. A sheet the team links to the board (Board ▾ → Link to board) keeps its own columns, rows and formatting. What changes is how a mapped cell looks and edits: the same status pill, people picker, type pill, pillar chip, date picker and link labels as the Content sheet. Switch between Before and After, then click the mapped cells.

How mapped cells work

  • Stage: the status pill with its dot. Click or press Enter for the stages you may move to; the others show who holds them.
  • Assignee: avatar and name; the people picker searches the team.
  • Content type: the coloured pill; its menu lists the workspace's types.
  • Pillar / theme: a chip; its menu lists the task's theme's pillars.
  • Due date: a date picker; past dates read red, as on the board.
  • Links (brief, design, video, final): the site's name ("Google Docs", "claude.ai"), or "+ link" to add one.
  • Typing still works: type a name, a stage or a date and it is matched, as today. Paste, Fill, Delete and undo are unchanged.

What stays the same

  • The sheet's own columns, row order, row heights, formatting, comments and frozen panes.
  • Unmapped columns are ordinary cells (Caption here).
  • Rows that aren't linked show plain text, with the "Not linked" note in the Task cell.
  • Who may change what: the same rules as the board and the Content sheet. A cell you can't change shows 🔒 and why.
  • The theme grouping, summary bar and Content-only buttons stay on the Content sheet.

Phones: the same controls open as bottom sheets.
