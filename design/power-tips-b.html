Ctrl+Shift+1 and Custom

  • Ctrl+Shift+1 sets Number: two decimals with a thousands separator.
  • Custom takes Excel's codes: 0, #, separators, decimals, text in quotes, %, sections for negatives and zero.
  • One comma scales by a thousand, two by a million: 1,250,000 shows as 1.3M; the cell still holds 1250000 (sums and sorts use it).
  • ;;; hides a value; the cell bar still shows it.
  • A code the sheet can't read is refused with a note, never saved half-read.

Group and the status bar

  • Shift+Alt+→ groups the selected rows or columns (Data › Group); Shift+Alt+← ungroups. Up to 8 levels.
  • The bar beside the row numbers (and above the letters for columns) has − and +, and the level buttons 1 2 … collapse to a level. Kept with the sheet after a reload.
  • Right-click the status bar: Customize Status Bar, as Excel, with Numerical Count, Minimum and Maximum to add; your choice is kept for you.
