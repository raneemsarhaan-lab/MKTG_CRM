3 · Switch features off for a small brand

The Strategy Community doesn't need platforms, hours or SLA. An admin switches them off. Those fields disappear for that brand's tasks everywhere, including team Spaces, while Forefront keeps them. Switching back on brings every earlier value back.

4 · Link a task, or create one, right where you type

You don't go to a menu to make a task. You type in a sheet row or on a calendar day, and Link task appears under what you typed. It opens the task list, already searching for your words. Pick an existing task to link it, or choose Create new task at the bottom. That's where you choose Simple or Pipeline.

What to confirm

These are the calls this design makes where the spec leaves room. Approve them as they are, or tell me which to change.

  1. Team words show alone; the board name is on hover.

    The Video Space's List says "Editing", and hovering it says "D-In Progress". The alternative shows both, "Editing · D-In Progress", which is clearer for newcomers but busier.

  2. Status words open from a button in the Space header.

    Only admins and superusers see the button. Users see the words but can't change them.

  3. Simple statuses get team words too.

    Planning could say "Backlog, Doing, Shipped". The three statuses keep their own order and behaviour.

  4. The kind is picked in the create form and never changes.

    The Space's default preselects it (Planning: Simple; the others: Pipeline). To change kind later, a member creates a new task.

  5. Simple tasks get three dashed columns after Published on every Board.

    They appear only when simple tasks are in view. The Kind filter (All, Pipeline, Simple) sits in the Board header and in saved views.

  6. A switched-off field shows as an empty hatched cell in mixed views.

    The task panel and the create form leave it out entirely for that brand. Filters and bulk actions skip that brand's tasks for that field.

  7. The C-Check switch only affects new tasks.

    Existing tasks keep their own 10 or 11 stages, as the spec requires.

  8. Link task appears under what you type, in sheets and on the calendar.

    It replaces going through the Board ▾ menu for one row. The menu stays for linking or creating many rows at once.

  9. Create new task, inside the task list, is where you pick Simple or Pipeline.

    It starts on your Space's default (Planning: Simple, the others: Pipeline), with your words as the name, you as the assignee and, on the calendar, that day as the due date.

  10. On the calendar, linking an existing task moves its due date to that day.

    The alternative is to only show it there without changing the date, which a calendar can't really do.
