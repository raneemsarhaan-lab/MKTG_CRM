Video sheet: themes and board-linked columns

Two additions to the merged Video sheet. Themes: videos group under their theme, like the Content sheet (Theme › Video › clips), with "+ Theme" in the bar and a theme chip on each video to move it. Board-linked columns: any extra column can follow a board field, so its cells show and save the task's value.

Try the column menu: the open menu on column O shows "Link to a board field…" and the fields it offers. Columns that follow the board carry a small link badge.

Themes
  • Videos group under their theme, like Content (Theme › Video › clips); videos without one sit under "No theme".
  • "+ Theme" in the bar makes a theme (admins, as on Content). The ◆ chip on a video moves it to another theme.
  • Group by in Columns still offers Stage and Editor instead.
Board-linked columns
  • A column's menu offers "Link to a board field…": Priority, Platform, Theme, Pillar, Owner, Due date.
  • Its cells then show the clip's own value and save to the task, with the board's rules; a 🔗 badge marks it.
  • "Unlink" turns it back into a plain sheet column, keeping its values.
