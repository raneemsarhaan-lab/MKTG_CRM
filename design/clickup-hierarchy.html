Spec 021 · Preview of what we're building now

Momentum Spaces

ClickUp's way of organizing work, built on Momentum's pipeline. Brand Spaces hold team Folders, and team Folders hold Lists. Any level can open as a list, board, sheet, calendar or workload. Each team names the pipeline stages in its own words, outside the Board, as the sheets already do. Work that isn't a deliverable, such as planning, is a simple task with three statuses. Each brand can switch off the features it doesn't use. Click around: the data is sample data.

US1 Brand › Team › List US2 Views at any level US3 Status words US4 Space features US5 Pipeline and simple tasks Later: richer tasks · Gantt · sprints · goals · docs · forms · automations

Momentum vs ClickUp: what this spec covers

The full parity map is in the spec. This is the short version.

This spec US1–US5

  • Space per brand, Folder per team, Subfolders, Lists
  • Private Spaces, Folders and Lists
  • List, Board, Table, Calendar and Workload views on any level
  • Everything view
  • Status words mapped to the pipeline stages, inherited from the Folder; Boards keep the original process
  • Feature switches per Space
  • Two kinds of task: pipeline (the 11 stages, SLA) and simple (To Do › In progress › Done)
