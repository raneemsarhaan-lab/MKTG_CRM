What happens behind the click

  1. WaitingChat tells MomentumWhich message, who clicked, and their Google email.
  2. WaitingIs Momentum set up?The app's Google settings are in Momentum's hosting.
  3. WaitingReally from Google?Every request is signed by Google. Forged ones are dropped.
  4. WaitingWho is this?The Google email is matched to a Momentum member.
  5. WaitingTask, onceOwner: the sender. Assignee: the clicker. One per message.

Nothing else in the space is read or stored: only the one message that was clicked. The reply always comes back within a few seconds, so no click ends in silence. Brand, type and due date are left for you to fill in on the board; "by Thursday" in the message is not read as a date.
